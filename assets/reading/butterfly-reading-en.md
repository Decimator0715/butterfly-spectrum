# Butterfly spectrum: a reading guide

Public reading edition, 2 November 2026. Based on the local chapter notes for
Indubala I. Satija, *Butterfly in the Quantum World* (2016), especially chapters
6, 9 and 10. This is an independently written summary, not a copy of the book.

## Read the animation

The left panel collects allowed energies as magnetic flux changes. The right
panel resolves energies along the momentum path ky = kx at the current flux.
Matching colored gap bars refer to the same global energy interval. The red
column projects the selected red rank branch, not the density of states.
The complete black background is visible from the beginning in the current
29 October videos. A scan in flux is a parameter sweep, not real-time dynamics.

## Separate labels from open gaps

With reduced flux alpha = p/q and r occupied bands, the integer candidates obey

\[\nu=r/q=m\alpha+g,\qquad pm+qg=r.\]

If (m0,g0) is a solution, so is (m0+nq,g0-np). The integer equation alone does
not select the physical Chern branch. It also does not determine whether a gap
is open, how wide it is, or its absolute energy.

The present nearest-neighbor square-lattice convention shows an independent
target of order |m| only for q > 2|m| and only when its computed global gap is
open. At q = 2|m| the central gap closes. Smaller denominators give a lower-order
representative or no internal gap. This rule is not a general prescription for
triangular lattices or models with additional hopping terms.

## Direct separation and a global gap

For ordered bands,

\[d_r=\min_{\mathbf k}(E_{r+1}-E_r),\qquad
\Delta_r=\min_{\mathbf k}E_{r+1}-\max_{\mathbf k}E_r.\]

Positive direct separation isolates a rank-r subspace at each momentum.
A positive global gap permits one fixed Fermi energy between the two bands at
all momenta. These statements must not be conflated. The animation reports the
global width; a jump in a selected colored branch is not a gap measurement.

## What the branch colors establish

The animation uses zero-coupling rank continuation: it selects the coupled
eigenvalue at the energy rank of a chosen uncoupled momentum replica, separately
at each momentum. This neither tracks an eigenvector through a coupling sweep
nor directly tests its central momentum weight. The colored branch is therefore
not a validated wavefunction-based IEB. Replica indices and gap labels are
different objects, especially when indices coincide modulo q.

## A useful reading route

Chapter 6: Harper model, rational flux, spectral sets and finite resolution.
Chapters 8–9: Berry phases, isolated subspaces, orientation and Chern numbers.
Chapter 10: gap labels, integer candidates and Wannier lines.
Chapters 2–4: Farey arithmetic and specific hierarchical constructions.
Chapter 12: experimental evidence as reviewed in 2016, not a current survey.

Workflow: specify model and flux units → identify the spectral gap → list integer
candidates → select the physical branch with independent evidence → interpret
the response under its filling and sign conventions.
